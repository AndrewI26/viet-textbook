### Likes, wants and shopping

Vietnamese word order is the same as English: *who* + *does* + *what*. Verbs never change form.

<sentence-builder vi="{who} {verb} {thing}." en="{who} {verb} {thing}.">
who: Tôi = I | Bạn = You
verb: thích = like | muốn = want | mua = buy
thing: phở = pho | xoài = mangoes | trà = tea | sách = books
</sentence-builder>

<sentence-builder vi="{thing} này bao nhiêu tiền?" en="How much is this {thing}?">
thing: Xoài = mango | Trà = tea | Phở = pho | Sách = book
</sentence-builder>

## Sounds

### Every syllable has four parts

**start + vowel + end + tone.** Only the vowel and the tone are always there.

| Word | Start | Vowel | End | Tone |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[tiếng]] | t | iê | ng | sắc |
| [[ăn]] | | ă | n | ngang |
| [[người]] | ng | ươ | i | huyền |

### The five Southern tones

<tone-chart>
[[ma]] [[má]] [[mà]] [[mả]] [[mã]] [[mạ]]
</tone-chart>

| Mark | Name | Your voice | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| a | ngang | stays flat | [[ma]] ghost |
| á | sắc | rises ↗ | [[má]] mom |
| à | huyền | falls gently ↘ | [[mà]] but |
| ả, ã | hỏi, ngã | dips, then rises ↘↗ (the same in the South) | [[mả]] grave |
| ạ | nặng | low, with a small dip | [[mạ]] rice seedling |

- **Words ending in -p, -t, -c, -ch** only take sắc or nặng: [[hát]] (to sing), [[hạt]] (seed).
- **Where the mark goes:** a vowel with its own mark wins (*tiếng*, *người*). Otherwise, with two vowels and nothing after, the first (*hai*, *mía*); with a consonant after, the last (*toán*).

### Spelling rules

| Sound | Before i, e, ê | Everywhere else |
|---|---|---|
| *k* | k ([[kem]]) | c ([[cá]]), or qu before a "w" ([[quá]]) |
| *g* | gh ([[ghế]]) | g ([[gà]]) |
| *ng* | ngh ([[nghe]]) | ng ([[ngủ]]) |

| Glide | Nothing after it | A consonant after it | Nothing before it |
|---|---|---|---|
| "ee-uh" | ia ([[mía]]) | iê ([[tiền]]) | yê ([[yêu]]) |
| "oo-uh" | ua ([[mua]]) | uô ([[muốn]]) | |
| "ư-uh" | ưa ([[mưa]]) | ươ ([[nước]]) | |

### Consonant pairs

| Pair | The difference | Listen |
|---|---|---|
| t · th | no puff of air · a puff (hold your hand up) | [[tôi]] [[thôi]] |
| c · kh | hard *k*, no puff · soft, like *loch* | [[cá]] [[khá]] |
| đ · d | firm *d* · *y* in the South | [[đi]] [[dì]] |
| ch · tr | soft · tongue curled back | [[che]] [[tre]] |
| n · nh · ng | *n* · *ñ* in señor · *ng* in singing | [[no]] [[nho]] [[ngà]] |

Endings pair up by mouth position: **p ↔ m** (lips), **t ↔ n** (tongue tip), **c ↔ ng** (back of tongue), **ch ↔ nh** (middle of tongue). One of each pair stops the sound, the other hums.

## Words

### "You" and "I"

| Talking to… | Call them | Call yourself |
|---|---|---|
| a man a bit older | anh | em |
| a woman a bit older | chị | em |
| someone younger | em | anh / chị |
| your parents' generation | chú (m) / cô (f) | con |
| your grandparents' generation | ông (m) / bà (f) | con |
| someone your age | bạn | tôi / tui / mình |

Not sure? Use **anh** or **chị**, and call yourself **em**. For he or she, add **ấy**: *anh ấy*, which in the South often becomes [[ảnh]].

### Building numbers

- **11–19:** mười + digit, and 5 becomes *lăm*: [[mười lăm]] (15)
- **20–99:** digit + mươi + digit, where 1 becomes *mốt*, 4 becomes *tư* and 5 becomes *lăm*: [[bốn mươi lăm]] (45), [[hai mươi mốt]] (21)
- **Hundreds:** digit + trăm, with *lẻ* for a missing tens digit: [[một trăm lẻ năm]] (105)
- **Thousands:** ngàn (Southern); **millions:** triệu: [[hai mươi lăm ngàn]] (25,000)
- **Southern shortcuts:** *chục* for tens ([[năm chục]], 50), *hăm* for 20-something ([[hăm lăm]], 25)
