## How to use this book

- **Listen to everything.** Any word drawn like [[xin chào]] is clickable. Tap it to hear it said with a Southern accent.
- **Say it before you click.** Guess the sound first, then check yourself. That's how your ear learns.
- **Practise every day with Anki.** Each chapter has a matching section in the flashcard deck.
