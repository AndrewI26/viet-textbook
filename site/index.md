## How this book works

- **Hear every word.** Anything drawn like [[xin chào]] is clickable, spoken with a Southern accent.
- **Say it before you click.** Guess the sound first, then check yourself. That's how your ear learns.
- **Check your tones.** Exercises with a **Record** button listen to you, draw your pitch as you speak, and tell you whether the tone matches.
- **Practise a little every day.** Each chapter has its own section in the Anki deck. Ten minutes a day beats an hour once a week.
- **Look things up fast.** The [cheat sheet](cheatsheet.html) states every rule as a formula, with links back to the chapters.
